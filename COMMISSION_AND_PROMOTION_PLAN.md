# 📊 IWR Commission & Promotion Plan (Updated)

## Commission Structure — Gap Commission Model

When a property is sold, commissions are calculated using the **GAP method** (difference between current level % and previous level %).

### Example: Property sold for ₹10,00,000 (10 Lakhs), Area: 0-6000 gaj

**Important:** The "seller" is whoever sells the property — they can be at ANY rank (Business Associate, Adviser, Head, etc.). They get their rank's percentage as direct commission.

| Level | Title | Slab % | Gap Calculation | Effective % | Commission (₹) |
|-------|-------|--------|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Seller's Level | (whoever sold) | Their rank % | Direct | Their rank % | Based on rank |
| Next upline | (sponsor) | Their rank % | Their % - Seller's % | Gap | Gap × Price |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 10 | President Club | 2% (flat) | Always 2% | 2% | ₹20,000 |

| 2 | Business Head | 7.5% | 1.5% (7.5-6) | ₹15,000 |
| 3 | Dist. Business Head | 9% | 1.5% (9-7.5) | ₹15,000 |

**Example: President Club sells directly (0-6000 gaj slab):**
| Level | Title | Commission |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Seller | President Club | 14% + 2% = **16%** (₹1,60,000) |
| **Total** | | **16%** = **₹1,60,000** |

### Key Rules:
1. Commission flows **UPLINE only** (not downline)
2. **Seller gets their rank's full slab %** (e.g., Business Associate gets 4%, President Sales gets 14%, President Club gets 16%)
3. Each upline above the seller gets the **GAP** (their slab % - previous level's slab %)
4. **President Club** always gets flat 2% on top (whether they sell or someone below sells)
5. Only **ACTIVE** associates earn commission
6. If an upline level is skipped (no one at that rank), the gap accumulates to the next available upline

### How it works:
- If **Business Associate** sells → they get 4%. Uplines get gaps. President Club gets 2%.
- If **Business Adviser** sells → they get 6%. Uplines above them get gaps. President Club gets 2%.
- If **President Sales** sells → they get 14%. President Club above gets 2%.
- If **President Club** sells → they get 14% + 2% = **16% total**. No one above.

## Promotion System

### First Promotion (Business Associate → Business Adviser)
- **Criteria:** Sell 500 gaj of land/property personally
- This is the only promotion based on **personal sales**

### Subsequent Promotions (Business Adviser → Business Head → ... → President Club)
- **Criteria:** Each of the 3 direct downline Business Associates must sell 500 gaj each
- When all 3 downlines achieve 500 gaj, the Business Adviser gets promoted to Business Head
- The 3 downline Business Associates get promoted to Business Adviser
- This pattern repeats up the chain

### Promotion Hierarchy:
```
Level 0: Business Associate      → Sell 500 gaj personally → Promoted to Level 1
Level 1: Business Adviser        → 3 downlines sell 500 gaj each OR 2000 gaj personal sale → Promoted to Level 2
Level 2: Business Head           → 3 downlines achieve Adviser criteria → Promoted to Level 3
Level 3: Dist. Business Head     → Same pattern → Level 4
Level 4: State Business Head     → Same pattern → Level 5
Level 5: Regional Business Head  → Same pattern → Level 6
Level 6: National Business Head  → Same pattern → Level 7
Level 7: Vice President Sales    → Same pattern → Level 8
Level 8: President Sales         → Same pattern → Level 9
Level 9: President Club          → Top level (no further promotion)
```

### Special Rules:
- **President Club** can directly add anyone under them (no criteria to add people)
- After first promotion, each person can add **3 Business Associates** under them
- Promotion cascades: when someone is promoted, their downlines also get promoted one level

## Summary of Changes from Previous Model

| Aspect | Old Model | New Model |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Commission calculation | Direct percentage per level | **Gap method** (difference between levels) |
| Who can sell | Only Business Associate | **Anyone at any rank** can sell |
| Seller commission | Fixed 4% | **Based on seller's rank** (higher rank = higher %) |
| President Club | 16% cumulative | **Flat 2%** always (separate from hierarchy) |
| Promotion (first) | Not defined | 500 gaj personal sale |
| Promotion (subsequent) | Not defined | 3 downlines achieve criteria |
| Downline limit | Binary (left/right) | **3 direct downlines** after first promotion |
| President Club | Same as others | Can add anyone directly, no criteria |
| Packages | Required for registration | **Not needed** — removed |
